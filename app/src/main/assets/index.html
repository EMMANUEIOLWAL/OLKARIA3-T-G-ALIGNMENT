Formula in use

  • Vertical: R and F at 6 (rim sag-corrected). Positive = raise the foot (add shims).
  • Horizontal: uses R3 − R9 and F3 − F9. Positive = move the foot toward the 9 o'clock side.
  • Check: R6 should equal R3 + R9 (and the same for face) within 0.03 mm, or re-take the set.
  • Do vertical moves first, then horizontal, then take a confirming set.
